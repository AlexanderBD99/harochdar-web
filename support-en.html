Frequently asked questions

A place has closed or shows the wrong opening hours

Open the place in the app, scroll to the bottom and tap Report a problem. We'll look into it and fix it.

Why aren't all cafés and restaurants shown?

We show a selection of places we think are worth visiting. Missing a place? Let us know by email.

What does Spotlight mean?

Spotlight places are places we partner with. They have a wine-red pin with a star on the map.

How do I use an offer?

Open the place in the app and follow the instructions in the offer – usually you just show the app at the till.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete account in the app. Your account and all your data are deleted immediately.
