Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

In short
  • The app works without an account. If you don't sign in, we store no data about you.
  • Your location is only used to show places and offers near you. It is never stored.
  • With an account, your name, username and profile picture are visible to others who view your profile. Your saved places are only visible to the people you choose: everyone, friends only or no one.
  • Everything is stored in the EU, and you can delete your account and all your data directly in the app.

Who is responsible for your data?

The data controller is Här&Där, a student company within Junior Achievement Sweden (Ung Företagsamhet). Contact us at harochdar.uf@gmail.com with any questions about how we handle your data.

What data do we process?

Data Why How long
Your location (only if you allow it) To show the map where you are and find places and offers near you. Your location is only sent to our server when you search or view offers, and is never linked to you or your account. If you turn on Spotlight nearby, your phone itself compares your location with nearby places, also when the app is closed if you have chosen "Always". Not stored
Account ID, email address and name from Apple or Google (only if you sign in) So you can sign in. If you choose "Hide My Email" with Apple, we never receive your real address. You can change your name in the app. Your email address is never shown to others. Until you delete your account
Username, name and profile picture (account only) So your friends can find and recognise you. Others can search for your username but not your real name. Your name, username and profile picture are visible to anyone who views your profile. The profile picture is optional and is stored by us. Until you change or remove them, or delete your account
Favorites (account only) So you can save places and find them again. In Settings you choose who can see them: everyone, friends only (default) or no one. Until you remove them or delete your account
Followers, follow requests and blocks (account only) So you can follow others and become friends (you follow each other). Your number of followers is shown on your profile. People you block can't find you, follow you or see your saved places, and are not told that you blocked them. Until you unfollow, remove a follower, unblock or delete your account
Used offers (account only) When staff enter the place's code, we store which offer you used, where and when. This makes sure each offer can only be used once and counts towards your stickers. Wrong code attempts are stored to prevent cheating. Places only see how many offers have been used, not by whom. Until you delete your account
Stickers (account only) Calculated from the data above (how many people you follow, saved places and used offers). Unlocked stickers are shown on your profile. Until you delete your account
Reports about other users and about places To keep the app safe and the information accurate. The person you report is not told who reported them. We may remove inappropriate names and pictures. As long as needed to handle the report
Push token, language and notification settings (account only) To send the notifications you've chosen: new offers at your favorites, messages from Här & Där and follow requests. You choose which ones in Settings. When you sign out, we remove the push token from your account. Until you turn off notifications, sign out or delete your account
History (recently viewed places) So you can find your way back to places you've looked at. Stored only on your phone, not by us. Until you clear it
Anonymous statistics (only if you allow it) To understand how the app is used and improve it. The statistics are not linked to you or your account. You can turn them off under Settings → Privacy. At most 12 months

We never sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Legal basis

  • Contract: account, profile, favorites, friends, used offers, stickers and notifications are services you choose to use.
  • Consent: location, push notifications and anonymous statistics, which you approve and can turn off at any time.
  • Legitimate interest: user reports, reports about places and protecting the service against cheating and misuse.

Where is data stored, and who helps us?

Data and profile pictures are stored in our database at Supabase, in a data center in Stockholm (EU). We also use:

  • Apple – the map (Apple Maps), Sign in with Apple and delivery of push notifications.
  • Google – if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Expo – relays push notifications to your phone.
  • PostHog – anonymous statistics, if you allow it (stored in the EU).

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU. If so, this is done with the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Information about cafés and restaurants comes from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors).

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • know what data we hold about you and receive a copy,
  • have incorrect data corrected – you change your name, username and profile picture yourself in the app,
  • have your data deleted – you can do this yourself in the app under Profile → the gear → Delete my account, and everything is deleted immediately, including your profile picture,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (imy.se).

Contact us at harochdar.uf@gmail.com to exercise your rights.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. For major changes, we will let you know in the app.
