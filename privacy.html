Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

In short
  • The app works without an account. If you don't sign in, we store no data about you.
  • Your location is only used to show places and offers near you. It is never stored.
  • If you create an account, we only store what's needed for favorites, history and notifications.
  • Everything is stored in the EU, and you can delete your account and all your data directly in the app.

Who is responsible for your data?

The data controller is Här&Där, a student company within Junior Achievement Sweden (Ung Företagsamhet). Contact us at harochdar.uf@gmail.com with any questions about how we handle your data.

What data do we process?

Data Why How long
Your location (only if you allow it) To show the map where you are and find places and offers near you. Your location is only sent to our server when you search, and is never linked to you or your account. Not stored
Reports about places (e.g. "has closed") To keep the information in the app accurate. If you're signed in, the report is linked to your account. As long as needed to handle the report
Account ID and email from Apple or Google (only if you sign in) So you can sign in. If you choose "Hide My Email" with Apple, we never receive your real address. Until you delete your account
Favorites and history (account only) So you can save places and see the last 50 places you viewed. Until you remove them or delete your account
Push token, language and notification settings (account only) To send the notifications you've chosen, e.g. when a favorite place gets a new offer. Until you turn off notifications or delete your account

We never sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Legal basis

  • Contract: account, favorites, history and notifications are services you choose to use.
  • Consent: location and push notifications, which you approve in your phone's settings and can turn off at any time.
  • Legitimate interest: reports about places and protecting the service against misuse.

Where is data stored, and who helps us?

Data is stored in our database at Supabase, in a data center in Stockholm (EU). We also use:

  • Apple – the map (Apple Maps), Sign in with Apple and delivery of push notifications.
  • Google – if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Expo – relays push notifications to your phone.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU. If so, this is done with the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Information about cafés and restaurants comes from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors).

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • know what data we hold about you and receive a copy,
  • have incorrect data corrected,
  • have your data deleted – you can do this yourself in the app under Profile → Delete account, and everything is deleted immediately,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (imy.se).

Contact us at harochdar.uf@gmail.com to exercise your rights.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. For major changes, we will let you know in the app.
